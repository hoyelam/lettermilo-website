How it works

Listen, tap, and try.

Pick a short activity. Listen to Milo, tap an answer, or try saying a word. A grown-up can join in.

18 letter games covering 19 letters, 17 pictured words, and 15 basic sentences. Progress is saved on your iPad.

Privacy

No account needed.

Speech is checked on your iPad. Recordings and recognised words are not saved. The app sends crash reports; optional usage sharing is off by default.
